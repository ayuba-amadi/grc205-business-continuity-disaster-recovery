# GRC205 — Final Academic Submission

This folder contains the consolidated **Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery** assignment for **GRC205: Incident Management and Business Continuity (Week 21)**, based on the fictional flooding and power-failure scenario at Northbridge Skills Institute.

## Submission files

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| [Final assignment — Word (.docx)](GRC205_BCDR_Assignment_2025-GCR-12684.docx) | Editable academic report containing all six assessment tasks, calculations, tables, recovery planning, references and the AI-assistance acknowledgement. |
| [Final assignment — PDF (.pdf)](GRC205_BCDR_Assignment_2025-GCR-12684.pdf) | PDF version of the final report for convenient viewing and review. |

## Report coverage

The report brings together the six required components: **Business Impact Analysis; Recovery Objectives and Backup Design; Recovery Strategy and Budget; Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan; Recovery Schedule and Tabletop Exercise; and Governance and Performance Measures**.

Detailed Markdown working materials and supporting analyses are maintained in the corresponding [Task 1–6 directories](../README.md). The recovery schedule and tabletop outcomes are explicitly **modelled or simulated**, not results of a live incident or production recovery exercise.

## Submission note

These files are provided in the repository for documentation and academic review. **GitHub publication is not confirmation of submission to ICDFA E-Campus**; the official assessment requires submission of the Word document through E-Campus.
